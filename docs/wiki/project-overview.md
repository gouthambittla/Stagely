# Project Overview: Stagely

## 1. Problem Statement
In India (particularly rapid-growth nightlife and event markets like Hyderabad, Bengaluru, and Goa), the culture of live bands, solo musicians, and performing artists has expanded rapidly.
- Pubs, bars, and clubs seek weekly and weekend live entertainment.
- Wedding planners and private hosts need vetted live acts (singers, bands, dancers, acoustic duos).
- Today, booking live talent is fraught with friction:
  - Reliance on informal WhatsApp groups and middlemen agencies.
  - Fraud and band impersonation (unverified profiles taking bookings).
  - Requirement mismatch (customers not understanding sound/PA needs).
  - Lack of calendar and pricing transparency.

## 2. Product Vision
**Stagely** is a two-sided mobile marketplace connecting performing artists directly with event bookers and venues.
- **Audience 1: Bookers / Customers**: Venue managers, wedding planners, corporate hosts looking to discover, review, and book live talent.
- **Audience 2: Performing Artists**: Bands, solo artists, and performers managing their public gig portfolio, equipment requirements, rates, and 15-day rolling availability.
- **Audience 3: Platform Admins**: Gatekeepers personally verifying artist applications to guarantee authenticity before they go public.
